build a route optimizer that works inside any supermarket, even very big ones, whatever its layout. It uses as input the MAP of the supermarket with all the products localised in the map with the product ID and the short-name, and the list of products (id:short name) requested by a customer. The goal is to find the best route for the “delivery guy” that is preparing the basket and shipping the shopping list to the customer. Success looks like we can simulate step by step the route of the delivery guy, measuring in real time the KPIs: “time-to-prepare-basket” and “distance-covered”, and compare it with the list-order route (not optimized) so we can demonstrate that our optimization algorithm successfully saves more than 30% of time for the basket to be ready
